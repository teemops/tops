Features

Know what's wrong, and how to fix it

A list of problems with no fixes just hands the work back to you. Every check in teemops comes with the steps to put it right.

Daily scans, without lifting a finger

Every connected account is scanned again each day. New problems appear on their own, so nobody has to remember to run anything.

See what to fix first

Findings are ranked by severity. A health score from 0 to 100 shows where your accounts stand, with the breakdown that tells you which area to work on.

A fix with every finding

All 77 checks include remediation steps. Each finding also has a ready-made prompt you can paste into Claude or your coding agent.

Alerts when it matters

When a scan finds a new critical or high finding, teemops emails your owners and admins, and posts to Slack if you connect a channel.

CIS AWS Foundations Benchmark

22 CIS checks run alongside 55 baseline checks across IAM, S3, EC2, RDS, CloudTrail, KMS, Lambda, DynamoDB, load balancers, SNS and SQS.

Read-only by design

teemops reads your configuration through a read-only IAM role. It stores no access keys and never changes anything in your cloud.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What does the $1 get me?

Thirty days of the plan you choose, with everything included. We charge $1 up front to check your card, and the full monthly price starts on day 31 unless you cancel first. The offer applies once per organization.

What access does teemops need to my AWS account?

Read-only. A CloudFormation stack creates an IAM role that teemops assumes, with an ExternalId unique to your account. teemops stores no access keys and cannot change anything. Delete the stack and access ends straight away.

Which clouds do you support?

AWS today. Google Cloud, Azure and Cloudflare are next on our roadmap. When they arrive, a GCP project, Azure subscription or Cloudflare account will count as one cloud account on your plan, the same as an AWS account.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, any time, from the Billing page in the app. Upgrades apply straight away and are prorated. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the month you've paid for.

What happens if I need more accounts than my plan includes?

teemops asks you to upgrade before connecting another account. If you move to a smaller plan, the most recently connected accounts beyond the limit stop being scanned, but their findings are kept, and scanning resumes when you upgrade.

Does teemops fix problems for me?

teemops tells you what's wrong and exactly how to fix it, and gives you a prompt your coding agent can act on. You, or your agent, make the change with your own credentials, because teemops never has write access to your cloud.

Is a clean scan the same as passing an audit?

No. A clean scan means none of teemops' checks found a problem, which is a strong signal but not a certification. It does help you stay ready between audits, because the same checks run every day.
