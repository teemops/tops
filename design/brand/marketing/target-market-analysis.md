## Part 5: Recommendations

### Immediate Actions (Month 1-3)

1. **Focus on SaaS + Fintech + Healthtech only**
   - These three industries have the highest pain and fastest buying cycles
   - All are cloud-native and understand the problem

2. **Target CTOs and DevOps Engineers simultaneously**
   - CTOs: Decision makers with budget
   - DevOps: Influencers who will champion the tool

3. **Geographic Focus: Wellington + Auckland (NZ)**
   - Dense tech community
   - Word-of-mouth travels fast
   - In-person relationships possible

4. **Company Size: 20-100 employees first**
   - Fastest sales cycles
   - Self-serve ready
   - Less procurement bureaucracy

### Growth Phase (Month 4-6)

1. **Expand to Tier 2 industries**
   - Professional services, HR tech, agencies

2. **Expand company size to 100-300**
   - Introduce sales-assisted motion

3. **Geographic expansion**
   - Australia (Sydney, Melbourne)
   - Similar market dynamics, larger TAM

### NZ Market Entry Strategy

#### Phase 1: Wellington Beachhead (Month 1-2)

**Why Wellington First:**
- Tighter tech community (word spreads fast)
- Government contractor presence (compliance-driven)
- Security-conscious culture
- Easier to build relationships
- Lower competition than Auckland

**Actions:**
1. Attend 2-3 Wellington tech meetups
2. Target 10 Wellington SaaS companies for outreach
3. Connect with local AWS community
4. Offer free security scans to 5 companies for testimonials

#### Phase 2: Auckland Expansion (Month 3-4)

**Why Auckland Second:**
- Larger market (3x Wellington)
- More fintech and e-commerce
- Higher growth companies
- More funding activity

**Actions:**
1. Leverage Wellington testimonials/referrals
2. Target fintech cluster specifically
3. Attend AWS Summit Auckland
4. Partner with 1-2 Auckland dev agencies

#### Phase 3: Broader NZ (Month 5-6)

**Actions:**
1. Content marketing targeting NZ compliance keywords
2. Case studies from Wellington/Auckland wins
3. Consider Christchurch for AgriTech angle
4. Build referral program for NZ tech community
