## Problem Section

### Headline
**Your AWS Account Is Probably Exposed Right Now**

### Body Copy
Most cloud breaches aren't sophisticated attacks. They're simple misconfigurations:

- Public S3 buckets leaking customer data
- IAM policies with wildcard permissions  
- Security groups open to the internet
- Unencrypted databases accessible from anywhere

**You're one misconfiguration away from a headline you don't want.**

The average data breach costs $4.45 million. For SMBs, it can mean the end of your business.
